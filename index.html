Timing & rhythm

Tap with the click

After four count-in clicks, tap the pad (or press Space) on every click. Modo measures how early or late each tap is, in milliseconds.

80 BPM · Andante
Wear headphones so Modo hears your drum, not the click, and calibrate once with this on.
